Our Journey &
Grassroots Roots

From a handful of dedicated field workers in Kurnool to a regional movement supporting 1,20,000+ agroforestry farmers and 5,000+ AWD paddy cultivators.

Who We Are: Leadership & Dedication

B. Raghu Bhaskar

Founder and President

Sadana Green Society (SGS) was established with a conviction that farmer prosperity and ecological regeneration are mutually reinforcing. Working directly in rainfed districts of Rayalaseema, SGS recognized that dryland farmers needed drought-resilient economic anchors.

Today, SGS partners with state forest departments, agricultural universities, international carbon registries, and CSR institutions across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Karnataka.

Our 4-Stage Grassroots Methodology

01

Listen & Understand

We conduct village meetings to assess local soil types, water access, and farmer livelihood goals before recommending any trees.

02

Enrol & Baseline

Interested farmers register their land, and our field teams capture GPS boundary polygons and baseline vegetation cover.

03

Implement & Plant

High-grade certified saplings are delivered directly to farms, accompanied by hands-on planting and organic pit preparation training.

04

Monitor, Verify & Share Benefits

Regular field audits and satellite remote sensing track tree survival and biomass growth, unlocking periodic carbon revenue disbursements for farmers.
