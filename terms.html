Terms of Service

Guidelines, commitments, and institutional principles governing participation in Sadana Green Society sustainable agricultural initiatives.

1. Programme Enrollment

Participation in Sadana Green Society (SGS) agroforestry and AWD water management projects is voluntary and primarily targeted at small, marginal, and rainfed farming families in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Karnataka.

2. Farmer Responsibilities

  • Providing accurate land survey information and genuine land ownership or tenancy verification.
  • Proper pit preparation, planting, fencing, and organic irrigation maintenance for distributed saplings.
  • Facilitating scheduled field visits by SGS community mobilizers and third-party carbon auditors.
  • Refraining from premature felling or unauthorized sale of project trees during the agreed crediting period.

3. Society Commitments

  • Supply of certified, disease-resistant saplings from accredited forest and horticulture nurseries.
  • Free on-farm training in pit spacing, bio-fertilizer application, and AWD perforated tube usage.
  • Transparent distribution of audited carbon credit revenues directly to participating farmer bank accounts.

4. Inquiries & Dispute Resolution

Any disputes or grievances regarding sapling allocation, measurement protocols, or community disbursements shall be resolved amicably through the District Project Coordination Committee in Kurnool.

Sadana Green Society

Established in 2008 in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh under the leadership of Founder & President B. Raghu Bhaskar. Transforming smallholder livelihoods through scientific agroforestry, water-saving AWD rice cultivation, and verifiable international carbon credits.
