5 Water Facility & Non-Deforestation Declaration

English: Official Project Terms & Conditions

  1. The applicant must provide truthful details about the land and its land-use / tree-cover history from 1 January 2014 to the date of application in 2026.
  2. Land where natural forest or native tree cover has been cleared, felled, or converted during this period is strictly ineligible for the New Plantation Project. SGS field officers will verify satellite baseline imagery before approving sapling delivery.
  3. The land must possess an active and adequate water facility to maintain seedling survival through the dry season.
  4. Submission of an application initiates review; it does not automatically guarantee free sapling distribution until field verification is concluded.
