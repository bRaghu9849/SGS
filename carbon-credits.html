Climate Finance & Rural Impact

Carbon credits and their benefits

How planting trees and farming differently can earn carbon credits, and who gains when they do.

1,20,000 farmers enrolled in agroforestry carbon credit programmes
🌿
Field Auditing & Monitoring SGS Officer & Farmer reviewing plot biomass
Clarity First

What is a carbon credit?

One carbon credit represents one tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent that has been reduced or removed from the atmosphere.

Credits are created by projects such as tree plantations, agroforestry and low-emission farming methods. Independent standards check the results before credits are issued. Organisations then buy credits to balance emissions they cannot yet avoid, and part of that value reaches the people who did the work on the land.

Shared Value

Who benefits

Carbon programmes work best when farmers, partners and the environment all gain.

👨‍🌾

Farmers

  • A share of carbon programme benefits, as set in the programme terms
  • Trees that add shade, fodder, fruit or timber value over time
  • Healthier soil and better water use on the farm
  • Training and regular field support from SGS officers
  • Official records that document the farmer's role in the project
🏢

Companies & Partners

  • A credible way to balance emissions that cannot yet be avoided
  • Robust support for CSR and environmental sustainability reporting
  • Verified impact that is traceable to named farmers and mapped fields
  • Tangible, direct economic benefit to rural communities
  • A trusted grassroots local partner for planning and delivery
🌏

Environment & Communities

  • Carbon permanently stored in living tree biomass and enriched soil
  • Greater regional tree canopy cover, biodiversity, and pollinator habitats
  • Lower methane emissions from agriculture through AWD in paddy
  • More resilient and stable rural farming livelihoods
  • Stronger grassroots community awareness of climate action
Operational Methodology

How a carbon programme works

From the first village meeting to benefits reaching the farmer.

01

Enrol

Farmers and their land boundaries are registered with clear informed consent and legal baseline ownership documentation.

02

Implement

Certified trees are planted and nurtured, or low-emission practices such as AWD are rigorously adopted in the field.

03

Measure & Verify

Biomass growth and water levels are recorded on tablets, audited via satellite imagery, and checked against international standards.

04

Issue & Share

Verified carbon credits are issued by registries and sold, with revenue shared directly with farmers as agreed.

Farmer Safeguards

What to know before joining

Be clear on the terms

Carbon income depends on verified results, programme rules and the credit market, so no amount is guaranteed. Before joining, farmers should understand the programme period, their responsibilities and how benefits are shared. Our team explains this in plain language before enrolment.

Step-by-Step Transparency

Carbon Credit Journey & Price Cycle

From farmer enrolment to verified credits and income — explained simply in English and Telugu.

తెలుగు: రైతు నమోదు నుంచి కార్బన్ క్రెడిట్ల విక్రయం, ఆదాయం పంచుకునే వరకు దశలవారీ ప్రయాణం.

Price transparency: Credit prices are market-based and may rise or fall. No fixed price or income is guaranteed.

ధరల పారదర్శకత: కార్బన్ క్రెడిట్ ధర మార్కెట్‌పై ఆధారపడి పెరగవచ్చు లేదా తగ్గవచ్చు. నిర్దిష్ట ధర లేదా ఆదాయానికి హామీ లేదు.

01

Enrolment & Consent

Farmer details, land records and informed consent are recorded.

రైతు వివరాలు, భూమి రికార్డులు, అంగీకారం నమోదు చేస్తారు.

02

Planting & Practices

Trees grow or eligible farming practices are followed.

మొక్కలు పెంచడం లేదా అర్హమైన వ్యవసాయ పద్ధతులను పాటించడం.

03

Monitoring & Measurement

Field data, GPS boundaries and evidence are collected.

పొలం సమాచారం, GPS సరిహద్దులు, ఆధారాలు సేకరిస్తారు.

04

Independent Verification

Results are checked under the applicable project standard.

ప్రాజెక్టు ప్రమాణాల ప్రకారం ఫలితాలను ధృవీకరిస్తారు.

05

Credits Issued

Credits are issued only when requirements are met.

అవసరమైన ప్రమాణాలు నెరవేరినప్పుడే క్రెడిట్లు జారీ అవుతాయి.

06

Sale & Price

Credits are sold at agreed market price; prices vary by project, quality, buyer and market conditions.

ప్రాజెక్టు, నాణ్యత, కొనుగోలుదారు, మార్కెట్ పరిస్థితులపై విక్రయ ధర మారుతుంది.

07

Farmer Payment

The farmer’s share is calculated and paid according to the signed project agreement.

సంతకం చేసిన ఒప్పందం ప్రకారం రైతు వాటాను లెక్కించి చెల్లిస్తారు.

How can the value change over time?

More verified climate benefits can potentially create more eligible credits, but tree age alone does not set the selling price. Actual revenue depends on verified credit volume, sale price, fees and the farmer-sharing terms.

కాలంతో విలువ ఎలా మారుతుంది? ధృవీకరించిన ఫలితాల వల్ల అర్హమైన క్రెడిట్ల పరిమాణం పెరగవచ్చు. అయితే చెట్టు వయస్సు ఒక్కటే ధరను నిర్ణయించదు. వాస్తవ ఆదాయం క్రెడిట్ల పరిమాణం, విక్రయ ధర, ఖర్చులు, రైతు వాటా ఒప్పందంపై ఆధారపడి ఉంటుంది.
