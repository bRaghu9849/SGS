Terms of Programme

Operational guidelines, farmer commitments, sapling care protocols, and transparent carbon revenue sharing frameworks.

1. Eligibility & Registration

Farmers with title deeds, patta passbooks, or long-term lease agreements in Rayalaseema and southern Telangana are eligible for free nursery saplings and AWD observation tubes upon verification by SGS field teams.

2. Farmer Care Commitments

Enrolled growers agree to follow recommended spacing geometries, pit preparation, initial watering, and tree protection measures to ensure sapling survival rates exceeding 85% across the initial 24-month establishment phase.

3. Carbon Credit Disbursements

Revenue from international voluntary carbon credits is calculated based on verified biomass growth from satellite and field MRV audits. SGS guarantees transparent accounting with credit revenues paid directly into farmers' registered bank accounts.
