Sadana Green Society

Established in 2008 in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh under the leadership of Founder & President B. Raghu Bhaskar. Transforming smallholder livelihoods through scientific agroforestry, water-saving AWD rice cultivation, and verifiable international carbon credits.
