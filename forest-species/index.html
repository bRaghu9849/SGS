Forest & Timber Species

Eight proven timber and biomass species for farmland boundaries, block plantations, and long-term farmer capital creation.

Forest / Timber

Teak (టేకు)

Tectona grandis

🌲
Harvest Duration: 15-20 Yrs
Soil Adaptability: Well-drained alluvium, red loam, black soil

Premier timber used in luxury furniture, building construction, and export.

Forest / Timber

Melia dubia (మలబార్ వేప)

Malabar Neem

🌿
Harvest Duration: 6-8 Yrs
Soil Adaptability: Deep fertile sandy loam to red soil

Fastest-growing hardwood; high demand in plywood, paper pulp, and biomass energy.

Apply for Saplings →
Forest / Timber

Casuarina (సరుగుడు)

Casuarina equisetifolia

🌾
Harvest Duration: 4-5 Yrs
Soil Adaptability: Sandy coasts, poor marginal soils, saline tolerant

Excellent pole timber, scaffolding, paper industry, and coastal windbreak.

Apply for Saplings →
Forest / Timber

Pongamia (కానుగ)

Pongamia pinnata

🍃
Harvest Duration: Perennial
Soil Adaptability: Hard clay, rocky drylands, drought hardy

Biofuel seed oil, organic pesticide, and nitrogen-fixing green manure.

Apply for Saplings →
Forest / Timber

Neem (వేప)

Azadirachta indica

🌱
Harvest Duration: Perennial
Soil Adaptability: Any soil type, highly drought-resistant

Natural pesticide, organic neem cake fertilizer, timber, and traditional medicine.

Apply for Saplings →
Forest / Timber

Bamboo (వెదురు)

Bambusa vulgaris

🎋
Harvest Duration: 3-4 Yrs
Soil Adaptability: Moist loamy soil, canal and bund banks

Green gold for paper, furniture, handicrafts, edible shoots, and carbon sequestration.

Apply for Saplings →
Forest / Timber

Subabul (సుబాబుల్)

Leucaena leucocephala

☘️
Harvest Duration: 3-4 Yrs
Soil Adaptability: Neutral to alkaline dry soils

High-protein livestock fodder, paper pulp production, and soil nitrogen enrichment.

Forest / Timber

Red Sanders (ఎర్ర చందనం)

Pterocarpus santalinus

🪵
Harvest Duration: 20-25 Yrs
Soil Adaptability: Gravelly, well-drained rocky red soils of Rayalaseema

Precious endemic timber; legendary global export demand for musical instruments and carving.
