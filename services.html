Our Programmes & Services

Connecting farmers, land restoration, and climate markets through verified agroforestry and water conservation.

Program 01 · Agroforestry

Farm Boundary & Block Plantations

Agroforestry combines commercial tree crops with regular agriculture or livestock on the same piece of land, creating multiple revenue streams.

Farmers are guided to select species matching their land contour, irrigation level, and soil depth. Trees planted on bunds do not displace food crops, yet generate invaluable timber, shade, and organic leaf manure.

Bund & Boundary Zero crop-loss agroforestry
Orchard + Intercrop Early pulse & vegetable income

Program 02 · Climate Finance

Verified Carbon Credit Programmes

One carbon credit represents one verified metric tonne of CO₂ equivalent removed or prevented from entering the atmosphere.

Sadana Green Society conducts rigorous baseline land surveys, collects GPS perimeter polygons, and tracks biomass increment. When verified under international carbon registries, credits are issued, traded at market rates, and shared transparently with participating farming families.

1,20,000+ Farmers in carbon pipelines
Transparent Signed sharing agreements

Program 03 · Water Conservation

Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD) in Paddy

Flooded paddy fields generate significant methane. AWD interrupts this process by intermittently letting the water table recede below soil level before re-irrigating.

Using calibrated perforated field water tubes inserted 15 cm deep, farmers visually inspect root-zone moisture. This method reduces irrigation pumping electricity/diesel costs by up to 30%, maintains high grain yields, and generates high-value methane abatement carbon credits.

5,000+ Enrolled AWD paddy farmers
25-30% Groundwater irrigation savings
