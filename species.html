Botanical Catalog

Plant Species for Agroforestry

Timber, pulpwood, soil-building and fruit trees we promote on farmland, with their benefits, best-fit land and uses.

Forest & Timber టేకు

Teak

Tectona grandis

Premium timber tree for long-term income and generational wealth.

Main Benefits: Very high-value timber for furniture and construction; stores large amounts of carbon in long-lived wood; fallen leaves add rich organic matter to the soil.
Best-fit Land: Deep, well-drained fertile soils with moderate moisture. Avoid waterlogged or shallow rocky hardpan.
Gestation & Uses: 15–20+ years for timber harvest. High-end carpentry, veneer, and building frames.
Forest & Timber మలబార్ వేప

Melia dubia

Melia dubia (Malabar Neem)

Fast-growing timber for plywood and pulpwood industries.

Main Benefits: Extremely rapid growth; straight cylindrical trunk suitable for farm bunds and boundaries; casts light, filtered shade allowing crop intercropping.
Best-fit Land: Deep alluvial, red sandy loam with good drainage. Thrives with early supplementary watering.
Gestation & Uses: 6–8 years for plywood and packaging crates; 10–12 years for secondary timber.
Forest & Timber సర్వి

Casuarina

Casuarina equisetifolia

Hardy coastal windbreak and nitrogen-fixing boundary tree.

Main Benefits: Root nodules fix atmospheric nitrogen; exceptional tolerance to saline, sandy coastal soils; acts as a protective shield against coastal cyclones and sand storms.
Best-fit Land: Sandy coastal belts, riverbanks, and light textured soils. Tolerates moderate salinity.
Gestation & Uses: 4–6 years for poles, scaffolding, and high calorific paper pulp.
Forest & Timber కానుగ

Pongamia

Pongamia pinnata (Karanja)

Hardy nitrogen-fixing tree for green manure and non-edible oil.

Main Benefits: Enriches barren soil with nitrogen; dense root system prevents soil erosion on slopes; seeds yield high-value oil used in biofuels and organic bio-pesticides.
Best-fit Land: Poor, stony, saline, or waterlogged lands. Highly drought-hardy once established.
Gestation & Uses: Seed collection from year 5; leaves used extensively as rich organic green manure.
Forest & Timber వేప

Neem

Azadirachta indica

Multi-purpose medicinal, pest-repellent, and timber tree.

Main Benefits: Natural biopesticide; purifies surrounding air; deep taproots mine nutrients from deep subsoils; drought-resistant and hardy against livestock browsing.
Best-fit Land: Dry, rainfed, and shallow soils. Tolerates extreme Deccan summer heat and low rainfall.
Gestation & Uses: Annual seed harvest from year 4 for neem oil/cake; durable timber for farm implements.
Forest & Timber వెదురు

Bamboo

Bambusa spp. / Dendrocalamus strictus

Rapid-growing versatile biomass asset with annual harvesting.

Main Benefits: Continuous annual culm harvest without killing the plant; dense root network stabilizes canal banks and prevents gully erosion; rapid carbon sequestration.
Best-fit Land: Farm boundaries, stream banks, and moisture-retaining soils. Avoid deep dry sands.
Gestation & Uses: First harvest at year 4–5, followed by annual selective cutting for construction and craft.
Forest & Timber సుబాబుల్

Subabul

Leucaena leucocephala

High-protein livestock fodder and short-rotation pulpwood.

Main Benefits: Exceptionally fast biomass production; deep nitrogen-fixing roots rejuvenate exhausted agricultural fields; nutritious green fodder for farm cattle and sheep.
Best-fit Land: Neutral to alkaline soils; highly responsive to farm bunds and boundaries.
Gestation & Uses: Fodder lopping every 45–60 days; wood harvested for paper pulp in 3–4 year cycles.
Forest & Timber ఎర్రచందనం

Red Sanders

Pterocarpus santalinus

Legendary endemic prized timber of the Eastern Ghats.

Main Benefits: Globally renowned heartwood with unique red dye and medicinal resonance; highly protected legal commercial plantation crop; thrives in harsh rocky terrain.
Best-fit Land: Dry, gravelly, rocky, sloping red soils with excellent drainage. Native to Andhra Pradesh.
Gestation & Uses: 20–25+ years for mature heartwood development. Generational family wealth.
Fruit & Orchard మామిడి

Mango

Mangifera indica

Long-lived orchard tree providing substantial seasonal cash income.

Main Benefits: Commercial cash crop with strong market demand (Banganapalli, Benishan, Totapuri); large evergreen canopy stores carbon and shades the soil; flowers support bee pollination.
Best-fit Land: Deep, well-drained alluvial or red loamy soil. Requires protective irrigation in early years.
Gestation & Uses: Fruiting begins at 3–4 years; productive commercial life spans 40+ years. Fresh fruit and pulp.
Fruit & Orchard జామ

Guava

Psidium guajava

Hardy, early-bearing fruit tree suitable for high-density planting.

Main Benefits: Begins commercial bearing within 2 years; yields 2 to 3 crops annually under proper irrigation; rich in dietary vitamin C with guaranteed local market demand (Taiwan White, Lucknow 49).
Best-fit Land: Wide adaptability from light sandy loam to heavy clay. Tolerates moderate soil alkalinity.
Gestation & Uses: Early harvest at 2 years; regular cash crop providing frequent weekly income.
Fruit & Orchard సపోటా

Sapota (Chikoo)

Manilkara zapota

Continuous fruiting, highly wind-firm and drought-tolerant fruit tree.

Main Benefits: Produces fruit almost continuously throughout the year; thick leathery leaves resist high winds and coastal storms; tolerates poor and slightly saline soils.
Best-fit Land: Deep alluvium, sandy loam, and coastal tracts. Tolerates dry spells well once established.
Gestation & Uses: Fruiting starts at 3–4 years; steady cash flow through multiple harvest flushes annually.
Fruit & Orchard సీతాఫలం

Custard Apple

Annona squamosa

Tough dryland fruit tree that thrives on rocky, shallow wasteland.

Main Benefits: Leaves contain alkaloids not browsed by stray cattle or goats; requires minimal water; high commercial value during the autumn festive season (Balanagar & NMK varieties).
Best-fit Land: Rocky, gravelly, sloping or degraded lands with good drainage. Needs almost no irrigation.
Gestation & Uses: Fruiting from year 3; seasonal premium pricing in local and urban fruit markets.
Fruit & Orchard ఉసిరి

Amla (Indian Gooseberry)

Phyllanthus emblica

Medicinal, drought-hardy fruit with strong pharmaceutical demand.

Main Benefits: Very high vitamin C content; immense industrial demand for ayurvedic formulations (Chyawanprash) and juices; deciduous nature makes it drought-dormant in peak summer.
Best-fit Land: Dry, marginal, light and sodic soils. Tolerates drought conditions remarkably well.
Gestation & Uses: Commercial yields begin in year 3–4; winter harvest sold fresh or dried to processors.
Fruit & Orchard నిమ్మ

Lemon / Acid Lime

Citrus limon / Citrus aurantifolia

Steady weekly cash flow through multi-season fruiting flushes.

Main Benefits: Year-round fruiting provides farm families with continuous weekly cash; compact bushy canopy ideal for field boundaries and interspaces; Balaji variety resists canker.
Best-fit Land: Well-drained sandy loam or light soils. Sensitive to water stagnation.
Gestation & Uses: Bearing starts in year 2–3; essential staple with round-the-year kitchen and market demand.
Fruit & Orchard కొబ్బరి

Coconut

Cocos nucifera

Perpetual multi-purpose palm for irrigated and coastal farmland.

Main Benefits: Produces a fresh harvest bunch every 30 to 45 days; provides tender water, copra, oil, coir, and leaf thatch; long productive lifespan of over 60 years.
Best-fit Land: Coastal sands, river valleys, and irrigated red loam with high water table.
Gestation & Uses: Bearing begins at 5–6 years (dwarf-hybrid varieties in 4 years); reliable perpetual harvest.
Fruit & Orchard జీడిమామిడి

Cashew

Anacardium occidentale

High-value nut crop that turns marginal and sloping land profitable.

Main Benefits: Thrives on poor, infertile, laterite, and gravelly slopes; valuable raw cashew nuts with strong export market; hardy canopy acts as windbreak and stops erosion.
Best-fit Land: Sandy coastal soil, laterite soils, and rocky degraded slopes. Avoid heavy waterlogged clays.
Gestation & Uses: Grafted trees yield in year 3; valuable raw nuts harvested in spring for high cash income.
