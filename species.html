Botanical & Farm Guide

16 Plants We Promote

Explore high-value timber, soil-regenerating forestry species, and lucrative horticultural fruit trees.

Teak

టేకు
Premium Timber
Tectona grandis

Best for deep, well-drained soils with reliable moisture. Functions like a family financial savings asset.

  • High commercial value for furniture & construction
  • Stores massive carbon in durable heartwood
  • Fallen leaves enrich organic topsoil matter

Melia dubia

మలబార్ వేప
Fast Growing
Malabar Neem

Rapid biomass growth ideal for plywood and paper pulp mills, harvested within 6 to 8 years.

  • Early financial returns compared to teak
  • Light canopy permits intercropping in early seasons
  • Captures carbon at an accelerated rate

Casuarina

సరుగుడు
Coastal / Sandy
Casuarina equisetifolia

Exceptional tolerance to sandy, coastal, and salt-affected lands where other species struggle.

  • Fixes atmospheric nitrogen into depleted soils
  • Acts as windbreaks protecting delicate cash crops
  • Produces straight poles and high-grade pulp

Pongamia

కానుగ
Drought Hardy
Millettia pinnata

Hardy native shade tree thriving in dry, degraded, and rainfed areas of Rayalaseema.

  • Strong nitrogen-fixing roots support neighboring crops
  • Seeds harvested for bio-oil; seed cake acts as manure
  • Dense cool canopy lowers extreme ground heat

Neem

వేప
Natural Pest Barrier
Azadirachta indica

Universal survival across arid soil types, providing invaluable organic agricultural inputs.

  • Leaves & oil produce organic bio-pesticides
  • Termite-resistant timber for rural utility
  • Deep taproot draws minerals to the soil surface

Bamboo

వెదురు
Perennial Harvest
Bambusa & Dendrocalamus

Ideal for riverbanks, field bunds, and watercourses. Can be cut annually without felling the root clump.

  • Unrivaled root network halts soil erosion
  • Continuous harvests for construction and handicrafts
  • Exceptional biomass carbon sequestration

Subabul

సుబాబుల్
Pulp & Fodder
Leucaena leucocephala

Quick-turnaround pulpwood and protein-rich cattle fodder tree for rainfed and irrigated fields.

  • Supplies paper mills with recurring farm income
  • Nutrient-rich foliage for livestock feed
  • High-volume nitrogen fixation in farm soils

Red Sanders

ఎర్రచందనం
Heritage Timber
Pterocarpus santalinus

Native to the Eastern Ghats. Highly regulated and extraordinarily valuable heartwood tree.

  • Thrives on stony, poor, gravelly hillside soils
  • Legendary market price upon legal maturity
  • Preserves unique endemic regional biodiversity

Mango

మామిడి
Orchard King
Mangifera indica

The cornerstone orchard crop of Andhra Pradesh, including the famous Banganapalli variety.

  • Generates strong seasonal cash flow for decades
  • High industrial demand for fresh fruit, pickles & pulp
  • Expansive canopy traps high carbon volumes

Guava

జామ
Early Fruiting
Psidium guajava

Hardy, compact fruit tree that begins bearing fruit within 2-3 years of plantation.

  • Frequent harvest cycles ensure steady monthly income
  • Rich in dietary Vitamin C and household nutrition
  • Adapts comfortably to light and red soils

Sapota (Chikoo)

సపోటా
Continuous Bearer
Manilkara zapota

Evergreen tree that flowers and fruits through the majority of the year under irrigation.

  • Extended marketing season reduces market glut risk
  • Evergreen shade moderates orchard microclimates
  • Resilient against common seasonal pests

Custard Apple

సీతాఫలం
Dryland Specialist
Annona squamosa

Celebrated dryland fruit producing delicious harvest even on barren, rocky Rayalaseema soils.

  • Thrives with virtually zero artificial irrigation
  • Rapidly increasing demand from ice-cream and pulp units
  • Cattle and goats naturally avoid grazing the leaves

Amla

ఉసిరి
Medicinal / Value-Add
Phyllanthus emblica

Indian gooseberry with unmatched Vitamin C concentration and huge Ayurvedic pharmaceutical demand.

  • Exceptional drought resistance once established
  • High returns through drying, juicing, and pickling
  • Low fertilizer and labor maintenance needs

Lemon

నిమ్మ
Quick Returns
Citrus limon

High-demand citrus tree suited for irrigated plots, bund perimeters, and domestic backyards.

  • Regular weekly harvests meeting daily kitchen demand
  • Commands premium summer market pricing
  • Compact size allows high density boundary planting

Coconut

కొబ్బరి
Multi-Utility Palm
Cocos nucifera

Iconic tropical palm delivering tender water, mature copra oil, husk fiber, and leaf roofing.

  • Generates monthly revenue for over 60 years
  • Wide spacing allows intercropping of cocoa, pepper, or fodder
  • Long-lived palm with strong root carbon retention

Cashew

జీడిమామిడి
Export Nut Crop
Anacardium occidentale

Durable nut tree thriving on red gravelly soils, coastal sandy tracts, and degraded wastelands.

  • High-value export quality cashew kernels
  • Stabilizes hillside contours against monsoonal runoff
  • Cashew apple can be processed into beverages
