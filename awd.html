Alternate Wetting
& Drying (AWD)

A proven, water-smart irrigation method for paddy rice that saves up to 30% of irrigation water, reduces pumping electricity costs, and substantially cuts greenhouse gas methane emissions.

How Does AWD Work?

Traditional flooded paddy keeps fields continuously submerged under 5 cm to 10 cm of standing water. This creates anaerobic (oxygen-free) soil conditions where organic matter decomposes into methane (CH₄), a potent greenhouse gas.

In AWD, a simple perforated plastic tube (pani pipe) is inserted 15 cm into the soil. Farmers re-irrigate only when the water level inside the tube drops 15 cm below the soil surface, ensuring root oxygenation and zero yield penalty.

Key Advantages of AWD for Farmers

30% Water Savings

Reduces total volume of irrigation water needed per crop cycle by 25% to 35%, conserving dwindling borewell aquifers.

Stronger Root Anchorage

Periodic soil aeration promotes deeper, thicker root systems, preventing crop lodging during pre-harvest winds and heavy rains.

30-50% Methane Reduction

Periodic drying introduces oxygen into the topsoil, stopping methanogenic bacteria and qualifying the farmer for carbon revenue.
