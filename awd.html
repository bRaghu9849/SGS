Water Stewardship in Rice Fields

Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD)

Growing paddy with less irrigation water, lower energy costs, and significantly reduced greenhouse gas emissions.

Water Conservation Technique

How AWD Works in the Field

Traditional continuous flooding cuts oxygen to the soil, creating anaerobic decomposition that produces methane gas. AWD alternates flooding and drying phases without stressing the rice crop.

A perforated plastic pipe (field water tube) is inserted 15 cm into the soil. As the water level gradually drops inside the tube, air penetrates the soil, curbing methane production and saving irrigation cycles.

Farmer Guidance

4 Questions Farmers Should Ask Before Enrolling

Transparency is our core principle. Understand your project agreement thoroughly.

1. What is the farmer's financial share and how is it calculated?

Ensure the written agreement states the precise percentage of carbon credit proceeds allocated to the landholder.
